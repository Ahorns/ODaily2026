# ODaily 3D planet texture credits

The planet sphere maps and Saturn ring texture in this directory are by
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) and are
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The standard-resolution images are included without modification. They are
visual reconstruction maps based on NASA elevation and imagery data; colours
have been adjusted and unmapped areas may contain matching fictional terrain.

Files used: `earth-day.jpg`, `earth-clouds.jpg`, `moon.jpg`, `mercury.jpg`,
`venus-atmosphere.jpg`, `mars.jpg`, `jupiter.jpg`, `saturn.jpg`,
`saturn-rings.png`, `uranus.jpg`, `neptune.jpg`, and `sun.jpg`.
